The mechanism机制

How it works运作方式

One curve, one price rule, fully on-chain. No order book, no private allocations — every buyer trades against the same curve.

一条曲线，一套定价规则，完全链上运行。没有订单簿，没有私下配额——每位买家都面对同一条曲线交易。

1

Buy on the curve在曲线上买入

Connect an Agent ID wallet and buy $QQAI with BNB or USDT. USDT payments are converted at the real-time BNB/USD rate, so both currencies buy at the same curve price.

连接持有 Agent ID 的钱包，用 BNB 或 USDT 买入 $QQAI。USDT 付款按实时 BNB/USD 汇率换算，两种货币买入价格一致。

2

Price rises as it fills越买价格越高

Every purchase moves the price up along the curve; every sell-back moves it down. Early buyers pay less — the curve rewards being early, transparently.

每笔买入都会沿曲线推高价格，每笔卖回都会拉低价格。越早买入价格越低——曲线以透明的方式奖励早期参与者。

3

Sell back any time随时可以卖回

Changed your mind before graduation? Sell your $QQAI back into the curve at the current curve price (2.5% curve fee applies to buys and sell-backs).

毕业前改变主意？可按当前曲线价格将 $QQAI 卖回曲线（买入与卖回均收取 2.5% 曲线手续费）。

4

Graduation: two pools毕业：双池上线

When all 59.94 T on the curve are sold, the raised funds and the 19.98 T liquidity reserve migrate automatically to two PancakeSwap pools — QQAI/WBNB and QQAI/USDT. LP tokens are 100% burned, and open DEX trading begins.

当曲线上的 59.94 万亿全部售出，募集资金与 19.98 万亿流动性储备将自动迁移至两个 PancakeSwap 池——QQAI/WBNB 与 QQAI/USDT。LP 代币 100% 销毁，DEX 公开交易随即开始。

What do I need to buy?购买需要什么？▾

A BNB Chain wallet holding a Binance Agent ID, plus BNB or USDT to pay with. The Agent ID gate is checked on every curve purchase.

一个持有 Binance Agent ID 的 BNB 链钱包，以及用于支付的 BNB 或 USDT。每次在曲线购买时都会校验 Agent ID 门槛。

How does paying with USDT work?用 USDT 支付如何运作？▾

Your USDT is converted at the real-time BNB/USD rate at the moment of purchase, and you receive $QQAI at the same curve price as a BNB buyer. One curve, one price — the payment currency does not change the price you get.

你的 USDT 会按购买时刻的实时 BNB/USD 汇率换算，你按与 BNB 买家相同的曲线价格收到 $QQAI。一条曲线、一个价格——支付货币不会改变你获得的价格。

How is the price determined?价格如何决定？▾

By the bonding curve formula in the contract. The price starts low and rises with every purchase as the remaining curve supply shrinks; sell-backs lower it again. The starting price will be published here at launch together with the contract address.

由合约中的联合曲线公式决定。价格从低位起步，随着购买增加、曲线剩余供应减少而上涨；卖回会使价格回落。起始价格将在上线时与合约地址一同在本页公布。

Can I sell before graduation?毕业前可以卖出吗？▾

Yes. Until the curve fills, you can sell $QQAI back into the curve at the current curve price at any time. The 2.5% curve fee applies to sell-backs as well as buys.

可以。在曲线填满之前，你可随时按当前曲线价格将 $QQAI 卖回曲线。2.5% 曲线手续费同时适用于买入与卖回。

What happens when the curve fills?曲线填满后会发生什么？▾

Graduation is automatic. The funds raised on the curve and the 19.98 T liquidity reserve are paired into two PancakeSwap pools — QQAI/WBNB and QQAI/USDT — the LP tokens are burned 100%, and $QQAI starts trading openly on the DEX with a 2% buy / 2% sell tax.

毕业自动触发。曲线募集的资金与 19.98 万亿流动性储备将配对进入两个 PancakeSwap 池——QQAI/WBNB 与 QQAI/USDT——LP 代币 100% 销毁，$QQAI 随即在 DEX 上公开交易，买入 / 卖出税各 2%。
