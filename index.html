The whole sale runs inside the mint contract. There is no presale, no private round, no team allocation sold early — and no hidden terms. This is the complete flow:

1

Free mint opens — Nov 26, 2026, 18:00 UTC+8

Any wallet holding a Binance Agent ID can free-mint 16,500,000 QQAI for the 0.0001 BNB entry gate fee, up to 2 mints per Agent ID. Only 555 free mints exist. When they are gone, they are gone.

2

Paid batches 1–4 — the BNB ladder

Paid mint runs in batches of 1,111. Price rises +5% per batch (0.003 → 0.003472875 BNB), package falls −5% per batch (50,000,000 → 42,868,750 QQAI), and every delivery includes the +5% Agent ID token bonus. Proceeds split 80 / 20: 80% is held to seed the pool, 20% funds development — 15% buy back & burn + 5% team. Refund safety net: if the pool has not opened within 7 days of the sale start, any pre-pool paid buyer can return all tokens received from paid mints and get 80% of the BNB paid back — of the remaining 20%, 15% goes to liquidity and 5% to the team. The moment the pool opens, the refund window closes permanently.

3

Mint #4,444 — the pool opens itself

The mint that completes batch 4 automatically creates the PancakeSwap QQAI/WBNB pool in the same transaction: seeded with the held 80% of pre-pool proceeds and QQAI from the liquidity reserve, priced exactly at the batch-4 rate. LP tokens are burned 100% and trading opens at that same moment. No manual launch, no delay.

4

Post-pool — every mint buys through the pool

From batch 5 on, a paid mint does not print tokens out of thin air: 80% of your payment is swapped through the pool by the contract — a real market buy that lifts the price — and the contract tops you up from the sale bucket so you always receive the full ladder package × 1.05. 20% funds development (15% buy back & burn + 5% team) — accrued in the contract and released once per completed batch, never per mint. The sale runs until the 665,842,500,000 QQAI paid bucket is empty.

Fading price bonus

On top of the pool price, post-pool mints are priced with a bonus that fades as the sale matures:

  • Batch 17+: 0% — spot price, bonus retired

Agent ID token bonus — always on

Separate from the price bonus and stacked on top of it: every paid mint, in every batch, pre-pool and post-pool, delivers the package × 1.05. A plain DEX swap has no equivalent — this +5% exists only for Agent ID minters.

What exactly is the free mint?

Free mint: 16,500,000 QQAI — entry gate fee 0.0001 BNB. The tokens cost nothing; the gate fee is the entry ticket, charged once per mint. Each Agent ID can mint free twice (33,000,000 QQAI for 0.0002 BNB in gate fees, plus gas). Only 555 free mints exist in total, first come first served.

What do I need to participate?

A wallet on BNB Smart Chain holding a Binance Agent ID. The mint contract verifies the Agent ID on-chain for every mint — free and paid. Identity on QQAI is shown by Agent ID name, never by wallet address.

When does trading start?

Trading opens automatically in the same transaction as paid mint #4,444, when batch 4 sells out and the contract creates the PancakeSwap QQAI/WBNB pool with the accumulated seed. LP tokens are burned 100% at creation.

Why is minting better than swapping?

A swap pays full spot, suffers slippage and the 2% buy tax, and receives no bonus. An Agent ID mint receives the package × 1.05 (+5% tokens), is priced with a fading bonus of up to +5% below spot after the pool opens, and — post-pool — routes 80% of the payment through the pool as a buy that lifts the price, with the contract topping up any difference so the full package always arrives.

Where does the money go?

Of every paid mint: 80% goes to the market (pool seed before the pool opens; buy-through-pool swaps after), and 20% goes to development — 15% buy back & burn + 5% team. Trade taxes (buy 2%; sell 10% in batches 5–6, then 2% permanently) fund the same development allocation. All of it is published here and in agent.json — there are no other fees. Before the pool opens, nothing is released at all — the split runs once at the pool opening; after that, the development share is released once per completed batch.

Refund — what if the pool never opens?

The pre-pool phase is a full escrow: before the pool opens, nothing is paid out to anyone, and every payment stays whole inside the mint contract. If the pool has not opened within 7 days of the sale start (deadline: Dec 3, 2026, 18:00 UTC+8), any pre-pool paid buyer can take a refund: return all tokens received from paid mints (burned back into the paid bucket) and receive 80% of the BNB paid back. Of the remaining 20%: 15% to liquidity + 5% team — the 15% is held as a liquidity reserve and joins the pool seed if the pool still opens later. Free mints carry no payment and cannot be refunded. The moment the pool opens, the refund window closes permanently.

Can the supply ever exceed 1 trillion?

No. 1,000,000,000,000 QQAI is a hard cap enforced by the token contract: public sale 675B, liquidity reserve 225B, team 50B (365-day cliff, then 4-year linear vesting), reserve 50B. Unsold sale-bucket remainder, if any, can only ever move into the liquidity reserve — it cannot be re-sold or re-issued beyond the cap.

Are the contracts live?

Not yet. QQAI is in pre-launch: the sale opens Nov 26, 2026, 18:00 UTC+8, and the official contract addresses will be announced on this website at launch. Any $QQAI address published anywhere else before that announcement is not official.
